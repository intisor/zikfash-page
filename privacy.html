We respect the fundamental right to privacy guaranteed under Section 37 of the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended) and are committed to safeguarding all personal data entrusted to us.

3. Principles of Data Processing We Uphold

In accordance with the NDPA 2023 and NDPR, all personal data processed by ZikFash is governed by the following core statutory principles:

  1. Lawfulness, Fairness and Transparency: Processed with full disclosure to the user without deceptive or covert tracking.
  2. Purpose Limitation: Collected strictly for tailoring ledger management, client sizing, style prototyping, and transaction receipt dispatch.
  3. Data Minimization: Restricted to what is strictly necessary to produce garments, calculate fabric, and maintain client contact.
  4. Accuracy: Tailors can edit, update, or recalibrate client measurements and profile details at any second in real time.
  5. Storage Limitation: Data is retained only for as long as your shop account remains active, or until you initiate deletion.
  6. Integrity and Confidentiality: Shielded by modern cryptographic controls, secure HTTPS/TLS 1.3 transmission, and database Row-Level Security (RLS).

4. Zero Invasive Telemetry Policy

Your Privacy Belongs to You: ZikFash does not sell your data, monetize your client lists, or run covert commercial ad-trackers in the main application. We do not track keystrokes or record unconsented microphone audio. Our central analytics are strictly confined to standard technical service health checks and basic acquisition channel attribution (such as knowing whether you found ZikFash through Instagram, TikTok, Google, or artisan referrals).

5. Third-Party Sub-Processors & AI Data Handling

To deliver advanced computational features, ZikFash engages reputable, privacy-audited technical sub-processors:

  • Cloud Database & Hosting (Supabase PostgreSQL): Encrypted multi-region cloud data storage enforced with strict user-level Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • AI Neural Processing Endpoints: Fabric rendering and document OCR models process submitted photos ephemerally to produce the requested style preview or extract notebook measurements. Data submitted to our AI pipeline is not retained to train public commercial AI models.

6. Your Statutory Rights Under the NDPA 2023

As a data subject under Nigerian law, you possess enforceable statutory rights:

  • Right of Access: You can inspect and review all personal and business data stored in your ZikFash profile at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the unhindered right to correct inaccurate names, phone numbers, or client sizes.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may delete individual client records or request complete deletion of your shop account and all cloud-synced records.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may export your customer measurement database to industry-standard digital formats.
  • Right to Object & Restrict Processing: You may opt out of promotional communications and restrict non-essential processing.

7. Data Security Measures & Breach Protocol

We deploy robust physical, organizational, and technological safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your data. In the improbable event of a personal data breach impacting your rights, ZikFash will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within 72 hours of verification and promptly inform affected users in accordance with Section 40 of the NDPA 2023.

Part C — Fair Refund Policy Artisan First • 100% Protection

At ZikFash, we understand that African tailors and fashion designers work hard for every kobo. Our monetization is built on mutual trust. We maintain a fair, transparent, and user-centric Refund Policy for all AI Coin purchases and digital transactions.

1. Automatic Coin Restoration (System Failure Refunds)

Instant Automatic Coin Refund: If an AI Style Preview generation, Smart Measurement parsing, or Old Notebook Scan fails due to an API timeout, server error, connectivity drop, or unfulfilled rendering, the spent AI Coins are automatically and instantly refunded back to your shop balance. You are never charged for a failed AI generation.

2. Monetary Refunds for AI Coin Purchases

When you purchase an AI Coin package via direct bank transfer, debit card, or mobile banking, you are eligible for a direct fiat monetary refund under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate Debits: If your bank account or card is debited more than once for a single package purchase, we will immediately refund the duplicate payment upon receiving your transaction reference or bank statement screenshot.
  • Uncredited Purchases: If payment was successfully deducted by your financial institution but your coin balance is not credited within one (1) hour due to banking network or verification delays, you may choose between immediate manual coin crediting or a full 100% monetary refund.
  • Unused Coin Package (7-Day Cooling-Off Period): If you purchase an AI Coin package in error and have not consumed any of the purchased coins, you may request a full refund within seven (7) days of the purchase date.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Monetary refunds cannot be granted for:

  • Coins that have already been consumed to successfully generate completed, high-resolution style previews or completed notebook scans.
  • Complimentary free coins provided during onboarding or promotional guild grants (as these carry zero monetary cost).

4. How to Request a Refund

Initiating a refund request is straightforward and hassle-free:

  1. WhatsApp Direct Support (Recommended): Message our dedicated merchant support line at +234 911 932 0371 with your Shop Name, Phone Number, and Bank Transfer receipt.
  2. Email Billing Support: Send an email to billing@zikfash.com or contact@zikfash.com with the subject line "Refund Request — [Your Shop Name]".

All refund requests are acknowledged within 24 business hours. Approved monetary refunds are disbursed back to the originating bank account within 3 to 5 business days.
