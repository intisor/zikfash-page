What is ZikFash?

ZikFash is a free measurement app for tailors that works completely without internet data. Take customer measurements without typing, see fabric on styles before cutting, and send receipts straight to WhatsApp.

ZikFash: Made for Every Tailor

What features does the ZikFash app offer tailors?

Easy Slide Tape

Stop typing numbers on small phone keyboards. Just slide the tape measure with your finger to pick the exact size in seconds.

Tap Body to Measure

No confusion on where each size goes. Tap the chest, waist, shoulder, or neck directly on the body picture to save the measurement.

Smart Tools to Save Time & Mistakes

How does ZikFash AI assist fashion designers?

ZikFash helps you show clothes to customers before cutting, so nobody argues with you.

styler

Smart Style Picker

Choose neck, sleeves, and body styles step-by-step. It stops you from picking mistakes that don't match (like putting long sleeves on a sleeveless top).

style

Show Fabric on the Style

Don't guess how the cloth will look. Snap a photo of the customer's material, and ZikFash puts it on the style so the customer agrees before you cut.

assignment

Magic Bulk Orders

Sewing wedding or group clothes? Paste messy WhatsApp notes like "2 Senator 60k (paid 20k), Agbada 50k due Friday" and ZikFash creates all the orders, prices, and due dates in 1 tap.

Mistake Warning

Tired of cutting the wrong size? If you accidentally enter 14 instead of 41 for hip size, ZikFash warns you immediately before you touch a pair of scissors.

Stop guessing how many yards to tell the customer. ZikFash checks their body size and the style to tell you the exact yards needed.

Born in a Tailor's Shop.
Made for Every Tailor.

ZikFash was born inside a Nigerian tailoring shop, inspired by growing up in my mother's workshop and seeing her dedication as a master tailor of over 20 years.

After seeing hard-earned money lost to misread paper notes (cutting 30 instead of 38), forgotten balances, and style arguments, we built ZikFash to give tailors simple tools that work without internet.
