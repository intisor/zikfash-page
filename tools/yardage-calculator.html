Quick Reference: African Garment Fabric Yardage Guide

Bookmark this table for fast reference when clients bring material into your shop or asking what to purchase at the market.

Garment Style Standard 60" Wide Fabric Standard 45" Wide Fabric Key Cutting Considerations
Senator Suit (Top + Pant) 3.0 – 3.5 Yards 4.0 – 4.5 Yards Add 0.5 yds if client wants long knee-length top or has chest > 44".
Agbada 3-Piece 7.0 – 8.0 Yards 9.0 – 10.0 Yards Full grand outer robe drape requires wide cuts across shoulders.
Kaftan / Jalabiya 3.0 – 3.5 Yards 4.0 – 4.5 Yards Check ankle-length hem allowance before purchasing.
Trouser (Single Pair) 1.25 – 1.5 Yards 2.0 – 2.5 Yards 60" width allows trouser front and back panels to be cut side-by-side.
2-Piece Business Suit 3.25 – 3.5 Yards 4.5 – 5.0 Yards Always use 60" wide suiting wool or cashmere for jacket canvassing.
A-Line / Maxi Dress 3.0 – 3.5 Yards 4.0 – 5.0 Yards Flared hemline volume drastically affects required meterage.
