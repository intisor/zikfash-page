How to Install ZikFash on Your Phone in 2 Taps

No heavy Google Play Store download. Zero storage space used. Instant access right from your phone screen even when your mobile data is off.

open_in_new Launch App & Install Read Step-by-Step
smartphone

For Android Phones

Samsung, Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi, etc.
  • 1
    Open Chrome Browser

    Open Google Chrome on your phone and go to app.zikfash.com.

  • 2
    Tap "Install" or 3 Dots

    Look for the golden "Install ZikFash" prompt at the bottom. Or tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.

  • 3
    Select "Add to Home screen"

    Tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app"). Tap Add to confirm.

  • 4
    You're All Done!

    The ZikFash golden needle icon is now on your home screen. Tap it anytime to work offline with zero internet needed.

smartphone

For Apple iPhone (iOS)

Safari Browser
  • 1
    Open Safari Browser

    Open Safari on your iPhone and visit app.zikfash.com.

  • 2
    Tap the Share Button

    At the bottom of your Safari screen, tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing upward ⎋).

  • 3
    Select "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down the share options and tap "Add to Home Screen". Then tap Add in the top-right corner.

  • 4
    Ready to Tailor!

    ZikFash now launches directly full-screen like a native iPhone app without browser address bars.
